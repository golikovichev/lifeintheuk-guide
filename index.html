Life in the UK Test - free practice guide

What's on the test, where the facts come from, and a free way to practice.

What's on the test

24 questions, 45 minutes, 18 correct to pass. You sit it on a computer at a test centre, in English only, and it draws from the official handbook Life in the UK: A Guide for New Residents. You get the result on screen before you leave.

What it covers

Five areas: the values residents are asked to accept, what the UK is as a country, British history, how people live in Britain today, and how the country is governed and its law works. Every part maps to a section of the handbook.

Why the source matters

Test-prep material online is inconsistent, and some of it is wrong or out of date. Every question in the practice app behind this page carries a named public source you can check yourself: GOV.UK, legislation.gov.uk, the Office for National Statistics, or UK Parliament. When a source page stops saying what a question claims, the pipeline pulls that question.

Practicing for it

  • 534 practice questions across all five parts of the handbook, each with an explanation and its source.
  • A mock test in the real format - 24 questions, 45 minutes, 18 to pass - with a full review afterwards.
  • Spaced repetition (FSRS, the same model Anki uses), so what you keep missing comes back sooner.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere while you study.

Languages

The exam itself is English only, so the questions stay in English - practising them in another language would prepare you for a different exam. The explanations don't: they're available in English, Romanian, Polish, Arabic, Urdu, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

You can start without installing anything: the same question bank, with the answer, the explanation and the source under every question, runs in the browser at trynoeta.com/practise/life-in-the-uk. No account, nothing stored. The section pages are the same bank cut by handbook part - all parts listed here - so you can drill just the part you keep failing.
